    THE LOOP

    • Search kitchens, shelves, lockers and trunks. Loot is finite and stays looted.
    • Eat, drink, rest. Empty bars drain health. Rain and night drain warmth.
    • Fight carefully. Weapons lose durability. Swings cost stamina. Guns attract everything.
    • Craft bandages, torches and spears from the Pack screen.
    • Build a campfire outside a safehouse. Campfires cook food and keep you warm.
    • Objectives: search 3 containers, kill 3 infected, build a campfire, then reach Havenwood High after Day 3, 06:00.

    SKILLS

    Combat, carpentry, first aid, cooking, farming and survival rise with use. Higher levels unlock recipes and improve results.
